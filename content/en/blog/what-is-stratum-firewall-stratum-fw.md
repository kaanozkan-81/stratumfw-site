## What is Stratum Firewall (Stratum FW)?
**Stratum Firewall** is an **open-source FreeBSD-based firewall operating system** built from scratch, offering enterprise-grade security.

Unlike traditional firewalls, Stratum FW is built on a modern Python (FastAPI) API and the ZFS file system, rather than legacy PHP-based architectures. Its goal is to protect your network while giving you flexibility, speed, and most importantly, **the technologies of the future** (like Artificial Intelligence).

## Why Should You Use Stratum Firewall?
A firewall shouldn't just block packets; it should understand your network and communicate with you. Here are the key features that set Stratum FW apart:

### 1. Artificial Intelligence Support (MCP Integration)
The most groundbreaking feature of Stratum Firewall is its **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** support. This allows you to connect AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, or DeepSeek directly to your firewall. 
* You can ask questions like *"What attacks were blocked by Stratum Firewall in the last 24 hours?"* and analyze your network using natural language.

### 2. Features That Leave Traditional Firewalls Behind
*   **IPS (Intrusion Prevention System):** Inline blocking with Suricata 8.
*   **Web Filter:** DNS-based site blocking with 55 categories and enforced Safe Search.
*   **Web Activity:** Detailed network privacy tracking showing which device (MAC/Hostname) visited which site.
*   **WAN Failover:** Uninterrupted internet with multi-WAN support.
*   **Encrypted Backups:** AES-256 encrypted backups including secrets and certificates.

If you are looking for an `open source firewall` or a `FreeBSD firewall`, Stratum Firewall offers you a modern alternative. With its lightweight structure, faultless updates thanks to ZFS boot environments, and a sleek web panel, it turns network management into a pleasure.
