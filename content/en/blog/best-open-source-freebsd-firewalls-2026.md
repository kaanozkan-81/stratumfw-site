When it comes to network security, the **FreeBSD** operating system offers one of the most reliable foundations in the world, thanks to its superior network stack and pf (packet filter) engine. However, building a firewall from scratch on FreeBSD is complex. This is where **open-source firewall** solutions come into play.

So, which FreeBSD firewall solution should you choose in 2026? Here are the top 3 alternatives:

## 1. pfSense (Netgate)
It is arguably the most well-known FreeBSD firewall solution. pfSense holds a large market share in the enterprise world. However, in recent years, licensing disputes between the open-source community and the commercial company (Netgate) have pushed some users to seek alternatives. Furthermore, its interface (GUI) and management style have become somewhat dated.

## 2. OPNsense
Born as a fork of pfSense, OPNsense offers a more modern interface and a more frequent update policy. It emerged as a reaction to some of pfSense's commercial restrictions and has been highly successful. However, it is still built on a traditional PHP-based architecture.
