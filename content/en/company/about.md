## The journey since 2004

My name is **Kaan Özkan**, and I have been in the IT industry since 2004. Over the last 22 years, I have designed, built, and managed some of the most demanding network and infrastructure environments in the hospitality and cloud sectors.

My career started in system engineering, managing large-scale systems and hybrid networks at Fener Technology. I then spent years as an IT Manager for major resort chains like Maritim and Adalya Elite, where I learned how to keep critical networks running flawlessly under immense pressure.

## The "Zero-Cost Security" Revolution

The turning point came at **Ethno Hotels**. As IT Manager, I built a 48RU Tier-3 data center from scratch, deployed an 8-node Proxmox cluster with 50TB of Ceph storage, and architected a 10G spine-leaf campus network with over 1,500 Wi-Fi access points.

But my biggest challenge was security. Instead of paying hundreds of thousands of Turkish Liras to commercial vendors, I built a **zero-cost security stack** using pfSense, pfBlockerNG, and Suricata. We successfully blocked over 600,000 malicious domains and achieved deep packet inspection — saving the company a quarter of a million TL annually.

This experience changed my perspective forever: **Enterprise-grade security shouldn't require an enterprise-grade budget.**

## Why Stratum FW exists

I realized that the open-source tools I relied on (like pfSense) were becoming bloated, licensing was changing, and the community was being pushed toward expensive cloud subscriptions. 

I wanted to see if I could build something leaner. Something built on the rock-solid foundation of **FreeBSD and pf**, with a modern, API-driven panel. A system that could run on a 10-year-old Intel Atom processor and still deliver 10G of throughput without breaking a sweat.

**Stratum FW is the result of that vision.** It is the platform I always wanted to use.
