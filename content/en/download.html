Before you download

Verify the integrity of the installer after downloading by comparing its SHA-256 hash to the values shown in each variant below.

Release Notes

What's new in v0.31.1

  • All / Default / Custom rule views, pfSense-style coloured separators, and up/down ordering on Firewall Rules and NAT (v0.31.0 – v0.31.1).
  • Built-in TOTP Two-Factor Sign-In with local browser QR rendering, 10 recovery codes, and HTTPS-only Secure session cookies (v0.30.0 – v0.30.4).
  • Direct OPNsense config.xml, OpenVPN PKI, Unbound DNSBL, and full-span DHCP pool migration with unchanged client profiles (v0.27.1 – v0.30.5).
  • Force DNS per network with Active Directory exceptions, Dynamic DHCP Multi-WAN failover, and minute-accurate rule schedules (v0.29.0 – v0.29.2).
  • Per-interface dual-engine Suricata 8 IPS/IDS, 55-category DNSBL Web Filter, and built-in FreeBSD-ipfw dummynet bandwidth shaping (v0.30.3).
