**Last updated: September 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Stratum Security ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the **stratumfw.com** website and related services. We have designed this site — and our software — to collect as little information as possible. The policy below explains what we do and, more importantly, what we do not do.

## Our approach

Stratum Gateway is self-hosted software. We do not operate a cloud service, we do not manage customer data, and we do not require any account to use our software. The website exists to describe the product and to distribute updates — nothing more.

## Information we collect

### Website visits

The **stratumfw.com** website is a static site. We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks, or third-party cookies. We do not build user profiles. We do not sell, rent, or share visitor data.

Server access logs are maintained by our hosting infrastructure for security and operational purposes. These logs may include:

- IP address
- Date and time of the request
- Requested URL
- User agent string (browser identification)

Logs are retained for a limited period and are used only for security monitoring, abuse prevention, and service reliability. They are not used for analytics or marketing.

### The live dashboard demo (`/try-me/`)

The live dashboard demo runs against a demo instance. It does not require registration and does not collect personal information. Any data you enter into the demo is transient and used only to demonstrate functionality.

### The update server (`update.stratumfw.com`)

The update server distributes software packages and a manifest describing the latest release. When a Stratum Gateway installation checks for updates, the server receives the HTTP request. We do not log which specific installation made the request, and we do not associate requests with any installation identifier.

### Email correspondence

If you contact us by email, we retain the correspondence for as long as needed to respond and, where relevant, to maintain a record of the conversation. We do not share email addresses with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Information we do not collect

To be explicit:

- We do **not** use cookies for tracking or advertising.
- We do **not** use Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible, or any similar analytics platform.
- We do **not** use social media tracking pixels.
- We do **not** require an account or login to access any part of this website.
- We do **not** collect personal data from users of the software itself. Your deployment is yours; we have no visibility into it.

## Third-party services

### Google Fonts

This website uses fonts served by Google Fonts. When you load a page, your browser requests font files from Google's servers. This means Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. If you prefer to avoid this, you can block Google Fonts at the browser or network level; the site will fall back to system fonts.

### Let's Encrypt

TLS certificates for this site are issued by Let's Encrypt. Their issuance process is governed by Let's Encrypt's own privacy policy.

We do not use any other third-party services on this website.

## Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete information about you, and the right to object to certain processing. Because we collect so little information, most of these rights are moot in practice — but if you have a specific concern, contact us and we will respond.

For visitors in the European Union, the applicable framework is the GDPR. For visitors in Turkey, the applicable framework is the KVKK.

## Data retention

Server logs are retained for a limited period, typically no longer than 30 days, unless a longer period is required for security or legal reasons. Email correspondence is retained as long as necessary to provide support and maintain continuity of communication.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited information we do handle. The website is served over HTTPS, and our infrastructure is kept current with security updates. That said, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Substantive changes will be reflected on this page; we do not use notification popups or similar mechanisms.
