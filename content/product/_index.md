
## What Stratum Gateway is

Stratum Gateway is an enterprise-grade firewall and network security platform that runs entirely on your own hardware or virtual infrastructure. It consolidates the functions that traditionally require a stack of separate appliances — routing, failover, VPN, intrusion detection, content filtering, and traffic shaping — into a single, coherent system managed from one web panel.

Built on Debian and distributed as open source software, Stratum is designed for teams who need enterprise-grade security without enterprise-grade complexity.
