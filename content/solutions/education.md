## Why education networks are different

Educational institutions run demanding networks with tight budgets:

- **Content filtering is mandatory** — protecting students from inappropriate content is a legal and ethical requirement.
- **Mixed populations** — students, staff, and guests need different levels of access.
- **High reliability** — digital exams and online learning require 100% uptime.
- **Small IT teams** — one or two people often run the entire network.

**Stratum FW is designed for exactly this kind of environment.**
